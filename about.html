About Ravi Traders

Nearly 30 years of doing it right.

Ravi Traders is a frontrunner in automotive spare parts distribution in India. We focus on secondary sales and the distribution of two-wheeler components, connecting top brands with over 400 retailers across Eastern and Central Uttar Pradesh.

The journey

Started in college. Built on trust.

Ravi Traders began in 1996–97, founded by Ravi Pandey during his college years with a clear vision: to create a reliable and ethical distribution network for automotive spare parts.

The business started in Delhi's busy Kashmiri Gate market, one of the country's best-known hubs for auto parts. It soon expanded to Basti and beyond, and grew into a key player in two-wheeler parts distribution for Uttar Pradesh.

Today we run three warehouses in Basti and Lucknow, handle an annual cost of goods sold above ₹54 crore, and deliver to more than 400 retailers on a regular 15 to 20 day cycle. What has not changed is how we do business: clear terms, honest billing, and long relationships.
